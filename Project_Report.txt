REAL-WORLD DATA PROJECT
Retail Sales Analysis and Business Insights

1. INTRODUCTION
Retail businesses generate large amounts of transaction data. This project applies data science techniques to analyze retail sales and discover useful business insights.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
Retail managers need to understand which categories and regions generate higher sales, how sales change over time, and which customer payment methods are commonly used.

3. OBJECTIVES
• Analyze retail transaction data.
• Calculate sales, profit and order-level statistics.
• Compare product categories and regions.
• Study monthly sales trends.
• Visualize important findings.
• Provide data-driven business conclusions.

4. DATASET
The project uses a 500-record retail transaction dataset containing order date, category, region, quantity, unit price, discount, sales, profit and payment method.

5. TECHNOLOGIES
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, CSV, VS Code/Jupyter Notebook.

6. METHODOLOGY
Data Collection → Data Cleaning → Exploratory Data Analysis → Visualization → Findings → Conclusion.

7. KEY RESULTS
Total Sales: ₹572,636.64
Total Profit: ₹85,598.15
Total Orders: 500
Total Quantity: 1492
Average Order Value: ₹1,145.27
Top Category: Electronics
Top Region: Trichy

8. CONCLUSION
The project shows how data analysis can convert retail transaction data into practical business insights. Category and regional analysis helps identify strong markets, while monthly trends can support stock and sales planning.

9. FUTURE ENHANCEMENT
A future version can add machine-learning based sales forecasting, customer segmentation, a Power BI dashboard and automated reporting.
